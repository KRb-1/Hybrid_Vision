Tracking and Trajectory Prediction

A tracking and forecasting demo based on existing detection results. It shows how detected drones are assigned stable IDs over time and how their future positions are predicted.

What this module does

Tracking

Frame-level drone detections are linked over time using ByteTrack. Each drone receives a consistent track ID across consecutive frames.

Prediction

A Transformer-based predictor uses recent trajectory history and event-frame information to predict drone bounding boxes for the next 24 frames (about 0.8 seconds of future motion).

Visualization

The video overlays current tracking boxes and predicted future boxes, making motion trends easy to inspect.
